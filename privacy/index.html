Privacy policy

Published by Radif Partners · Updated 26 September 2026

This site is a set of static pages with calculators that run in your browser. That shapes everything below: the figures you enter into the baby cost calculator, the budget planner or the registry checklist stay on your device and are never sent to us, because there is no server here to send them to. What we do collect is limited to anonymous audience measurement, and this page says exactly what that means, who processes it and how to stop it.

Who is responsible

The controller for the data described here is Radif Partners, publisher of BumpWeek, reachable at contact@bumpweek.com. Full publisher details are on the legal notice.

What the calculators do with your figures

Nothing leaves your browser. Salary, due date, insurance deductible, nursery quote: all of it is processed by JavaScript running on your own device. Where a tool remembers your entries between visits, it uses your browser's local storage, which is readable only by this site on your own machine and which you can clear at any time through your browser settings. We have no copy and no way to obtain one.

Audience measurement

We measure how many people read each page, which pages they arrive from and roughly where in the world they are, so that we know which guides are worth keeping current. This is done with analytics scripts that set cookies on your device and that transmit a shortened IP address, page URL, referrer, device type and browser language to their provider. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding the audience of a free site; no advertising profile is built, no data is sold, and none of it is cross-referenced with any other source.

Cookies

The site sets no cookie of its own for tracking. Analytics cookies are set by the providers named above and expire within thirteen months at the latest. You can refuse or delete them in your browser settings without losing access to any part of the site: every page and every calculator works with cookies blocked.

Retention

Aggregated audience statistics are kept for fourteen months, the maximum retention recommended by European data protection authorities for this purpose. Nothing that identifies an individual reader is retained, because nothing that identifies an individual reader is collected.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives a request from your browser containing your IP address in order to serve the font file. Analytics providers receive the data described above. Some pages link to external sites; once you follow such a link, that site's own policy applies and ours does not.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction of, erasure of or restriction on any personal data we hold, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. In practice we hold no file in which you could be identified, so the usual answer to an access request is that there is nothing to disclose; we will say so in writing if you ask. Requests go to contact@bumpweek.com. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to your national supervisory authority.
