Built by a Dad. Backed by Real Data.

When my wife and I were expecting our first child in Dubai, I did what any finance person would do — I opened a spreadsheet. I wanted to know exactly how much this baby was going to cost us.

And I was shocked at how bad the information out there was. The most-cited data came from a USDA report that was last updated in 2015. Most calculators were US-only and didn't let you customize anything. The articles were vague and outdated. I couldn't find a single tool that let me input my actual situation — my country, my childcare choice, my feeding plan — and get a real number.

So I built one.

Two kids later, I've tracked every dirham, pound, and dollar we've spent. BumpWeek is the site I wished existed when we started this journey.

Who's behind this

My name is Sarah. I have an MBA from INSEAD and a background in finance. I'm a father of two, based in Dubai. I've raised children across different healthcare and childcare systems, and I've made every financial mistake a new parent can make — so you don't have to.

How we work

Every number on this site comes from one of three sources:

  • Government data and institutional research (USDA, Brookings Institution, ONS, GOV.UK, MOHAP, BLS, IRS)
  • Published reports from healthcare and childcare organizations
  • Our own family's actual, tracked expenses

I'm not a doctor. You won't find medical advice on BumpWeek. What you'll find is clear financial guidance for one of the biggest transitions of your life, from someone who's been through it twice.
